Ordering
Browse the shop, add pieces to your bag, and proceed to checkout. You can pay by card or supported digital payment methods. Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive an email with your order details and tracking info.
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, your order enters processing and changes are no longer possible. Contact us immediately at contact@inrl.co if you need to make a change.
No account is required to purchase. However, creating an account lets you track orders, save your wishlist, register the pieces you own, and manage what they play.
All prices are listed in USD. We accept major credit and debit cards. Currency conversion, if needed, is handled by your payment provider at checkout.
Shipping
We ship to most countries worldwide. Shipping availability and rates are displayed at checkout based on your delivery address. Some remote locations may have limited carrier options.
Standard delivery takes 5–10 business days depending on your location. Express options (2–4 business days) are available at checkout for most destinations. Orders are dispatched within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. You can also visit order tracking and enter your order ID to see the latest status.
Free standard shipping is available on orders over $150 to most destinations. The exact threshold and applicable regions are shown at checkout.
The layer
Every INRL garment carries a printed marker. Point a phone camera at it and whatever's attached to that piece right now plays — directly in the browser, no app download required. The owner controls what that is, and can replace it any number of times.
No app is needed. INRL uses web-based AR (powered by MindAR), which runs directly in any modern mobile browser. Open your camera and point it at the print to launch the experience instantly.
Yes. Register the piece to your account, then upload and publish a new layer any time from your wardrobe. Anyone who scans it will see whatever's live right now.
Yes. The print is screen-printed using durable, wash-resistant inks. We recommend following the care label (cold wash, air dry) to keep it reading cleanly. The layer itself lives in the cloud and isn't affected by wear.
Returns & exchanges
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached. Sale items and items marked as final sale are not eligible. To start a return, email contact@inrl.co with your order number.
Contact us within 30 days of receiving your order. We'll arrange a return of the original item and ship your new size once we confirm stock availability.
Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect your returned item. The credit will appear on your original payment method.
Email contact@inrl.co within 7 days of receiving your order, including your order number and photos of the damage. We'll arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

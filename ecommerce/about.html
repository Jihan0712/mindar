INRL makes a garment once, properly, from heavyweight cotton meant to outlast the season. What it carries is made continuously — replaced without anyone touching the shirt.

Our mission

The object stays still. The thing it points at doesn't.

A garment is finished the moment it leaves the factory. Everything about it — the print, the message, the moment it belonged to — is fixed at that point and stays fixed for as long as the shirt survives. Culture doesn't work that way. It moves weekly.

So we built the two halves separately. The garment, made once. The layer it carries, made continuously — and registered against the piece, not the order, so it keeps working for whoever is wearing it.

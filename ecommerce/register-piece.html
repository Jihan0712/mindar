The fast way

Scan the tag with your phone

Point your phone camera at this code. It opens the camera on your phone, then you hold it to the tag sewn inside the collar.

The tag is a small woven label, not the printed graphic on the back.

Or by hand

Printed under the size. Case does not matter.

What happens when it matches

Claiming links the garment to your account. You can then publish a layer to it, and replace that layer whenever you like. If you sell the shirt, the new owner claims it the same way and it leaves your wardrobe.

No piece with that code

Nothing in the catalogue carries that code. Codes are twelve characters and case does not matter, so the usual cause is a mistyped character.

Check these first

0 and O look alike on a woven label, and so do 1 and I. Try the other one.

Still nothing?

Photograph the tag and send it to contact@inrl.co. We can look it up from the picture.

No tag at all?

Pieces made before Aug 2026 have the code printed under the size instead.

Registered to another account

A piece can only sit in one wardrobe. If you bought this second hand, the previous owner has to release it — or you can ask us to move it, and we will check the receipt first.

Why we check

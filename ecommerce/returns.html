Made to order and shipped from the factory that prints it. That makes delivery slower than a warehouse and returns a little stricter. Here is exactly how it works.

Shipping

Where

13 countries.

Australia, Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, New Zealand, the Philippines, Spain, the United Kingdom and the United States.

Cost and time

Shown before you pay.

Every T-shirt is printed to order by Printful, then shipped. The shipping cost and delivery estimate for your address are worked out at checkout, before you pay.

Tracking

Follow it online.

Once your order ships, follow it on the Track your order page with your order number and email.

Returns

30 days from delivery.

Unworn, unwashed, tags on. Send it back and we refund the T-shirt — not the original shipping. Because it was printed for you, we cannot resell a worn one, so this is stricter than a warehouse brand.

Exchanges

Size swaps only.

We do not hold stock, so an exchange is a return and a new order. Message us first and we will cover the return postage on a sizing swap.

Faults

We pay, always.

A misprint, a seam, a print the camera will not recognise — send a photo within 30 days and we replace it. You do not post anything back.

And the video

It stays in
your account.

Return a T-shirt and we take the video you chose off it before it goes anywhere else. Sell it or give it away and it stays in your account, with your video on it, until you ask us to move it to the new owner — from then on, the video is theirs to change.

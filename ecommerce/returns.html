Made-in time is Printful cutting and printing your piece. Nothing is held in stock, so both halves are real.

Returns

30 days from delivery.

Unworn, unwashed, tags on. Send it back and we refund the piece — not the original shipping. Because it was printed for you, we cannot resell a worn one, so this is stricter than a warehouse brand.

Exchanges

Size swaps only.

We do not hold stock, so an exchange is a return and a new order. Message us first and we will cover the return postage on a sizing swap.

Faults

We pay, always.

A misprint, a seam, a marker that will not scan — send a photo within 30 days and we replace it. You do not post anything back.

And the layer

It belongs to the
shirt, not the buyer.

Return a piece and the layer you published on it is taken off before it goes anywhere else. Sell it or hand it on and the layer travels with the garment — the new owner registers it and can replace it themselves. Either way, what you uploaded never follows a stranger home.

What you are buying

A T-shirt, made to order, plus a link between that T-shirt and a video you choose. You own the T-shirt outright.

Your video

Your video is linked to your T-shirt, and the T-shirt is linked to your account. It stays there until you ask us to move it to someone else’s account. We host what you upload and can remove anything unlawful.

Your data

When someone opens a T-shirt’s link we add one to its scan count. We do not build a profile of who scanned, and we do not sell scan data. The camera image is processed on the phone; it is not uploaded or recorded.

For an order we keep what is needed to make and deliver it: your name, email and address. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

If you sign up in the footer, we keep your email address to send you InRL news and product updates. Contact us to be taken off the list.

Returns

Thirty days, unworn, tags on. Full terms on the Shipping and Returns page.

Liability

The full limitation of liability will be written out in the binding version of these terms.

Changes

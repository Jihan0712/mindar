What you are buying

A garment, made to order, plus a link between that garment and a piece of content you control. You own the shirt outright.

The layer

The layer is registered against the piece, not against you. Sell the shirt and the link goes with it. We host what you upload and can remove anything unlawful.

Your data

A scan resolves a link. We count that a scan happened. We do not build a profile of who scanned, and we do not sell scan data. The camera runs on the phone — nothing is uploaded or recorded.

For an order we keep what is needed to make and deliver it: your name, email and address. Card details go to Stripe and never reach us.

Returns

Thirty days, unworn, tags on. Full terms on the Returns page.

Liability

The full limitation of liability will be written out in the binding version of these terms.

Changes

If these terms change materially we email everyone with an account before it takes effect.

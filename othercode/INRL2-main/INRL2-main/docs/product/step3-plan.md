# Step 3 plan: publish → marker → scan

Written 2026-10-07 (overnight session). Goal of the step: **someone makes a
scene, publishes it, gets their own marker, and anyone who scans that marker
(with InRL's scan button or the phone's normal camera) sees the scene in AR.**

Rules for the night: no paid services, no new accounts or keys, never break
what's live (InStudio, My scenes, cloud saving, AR pages). Product choices
that are Nico's are made provisionally and listed in STATUS.md under
"Decisions to confirm".

## Pieces, in order

### 1. Accounts (sign-in) — needed by everything else
- **Sign-in method: passkeys** (Face ID / Touch ID, saved in iCloud Keychain).
  No passwords, no email service, no keys or paid services. Google / Apple /
  email buttons can be added beside it later (each needs a key or account
  Nico sets up). Built so a second method is a new endpoint, not a rewrite.
- Screens follow Figma's onboarding row (F): splash with the sign-in choice →
  profile (full name, username) → hello. Email / phone / code / password
  screens are replaced by the passkey prompt.
- **This phone joins the account.** The phone already has a private key used
  for cloud saving; signing in links it to the account and moves the phone's
  scenes into the account. Nothing else changes for cloud saving (same
  header, same code). Signing out gives the phone a fresh key.
- The iPhone floor test app keeps working (it uses the phone key, which now
  points at the account).
- Needs: D1 tables `accounts`, `credentials`, `challenges`, `phones`; worker
  endpoints `/api/auth/*`, `/api/me`. Nothing from Nico.

### 2. Personal marker
- Every account gets one marker: **artwork generated by InRL** from the
  account (seeded, so it is unique and stable), in the design-system palette,
  following the marker guide (busy, irregular, high contrast, detail to the
  edges), with the **QR code** in a white card in the middle and the
  **@username** on it. "QR identifies, artwork tracks."
- The QR is a short link: `https://<site>/m/<code>`.
- The tracking file (.mind) is made **on the phone** when the marker is
  created (MindAR's compiler in the browser, a few seconds), checked against
  the quality gate (≥ 250 matching points), and stored in R2 with the image.
- Profile → Marker tab: the marker, "Save / Share" (iOS share sheet: Save
  Image, Print), "Edit your Marker" (which post it shows), "New design"
  (warns that printed copies stop working).
- Needs: QR drawing in the browser (`qrcode`, already used by scripts),
  D1 table `markers`, R2 keys `markers/<code>.png|.mind`.

### 3. Publish
- The editor's → arrow opens **New Post** (Figma E18r2/E18r3): picture of the
  scene, caption (0/150), **Attach to Your Marker** (on by default), **Publish
  Post** → **Published Successfully** (E19r3) with "See it on your marker" /
  "Share". Music row hidden (no music yet).
- Not signed in → the sign-in sheet first, then back to New Post.
- A post is a **copy of the scene at that moment** (editing the scene later
  doesn't change the post; publishing again makes a new post).
- Media used by posts are never cleaned up while a post uses them.
- Profile → Posts grid; delete a post.
- Needs: D1 table `posts`; `/api/posts`.

### 4. Scan
- **Phone's normal camera:** the QR opens `/m/<code>` in Safari: the owner's
  name and the post, "Open camera" → AR on that marker with the attached
  post (same tracker, anti-jitter, gyro, flat/upright as the preview). Swipe
  at the bottom through the owner's other posts (decision 2026-10-06).
- **In-app Scan button:** opens the camera, reads the QR inside the artwork
  (jsQR, in the browser), then loads that marker and tracks it in the same
  camera session. The test marker's QR keeps opening the test scene.
- Needs: `MarkerTracker` split into "open camera" and "track this marker";
  `/api/m/<code>` (public); jsQR (Apache-2.0).

### 5. Home and navigation
- Home gets the app's shape: Scan, InStudio (My scenes), Profile; the test
  pages move to a "Test pages" section. The full bottom bar (feed, search,
  notifications) waits for step 5.

## Tests (all automated, headless Chrome)
- Unit: passkey verification (signatures made with WebCrypto), accounts,
  phone linking and scene moving, posts and media clean-up, marker codes.
- `test:studio` keeps passing unchanged (InStudio, My scenes, cloud saving).
- New `test:publish`: sign up with a virtual passkey (Chrome's WebAuthn
  emulator), publish a scene, marker created and compiled, `/m/<code>` opens,
  fake camera showing **that personal marker** → scene appears; in-app scan
  reads the QR and finds the marker.
- `test:ar` and `test:look` against dev and live after each ship.

## What needs Nico later (not tonight)

- Marker artwork direction (generated vs. chosen vs. people's own art).

## Recommended copy

### Printed once. Change forever.

Never just one you.

InRL lets you add a video to your T-shirt to show the side of you you want to share, here and now. Change it whenever you choose, and let others watch it come to life through their phone’s camera.

**No app needed.**

**Primary CTA:** Buy now  
**Secondary CTA:** See how it works

## Adjacent media direction

Use the existing hero media area; no extra section. All content will be AI-produced by Marine. Represent the actual T-shirt faithfully using approved references, with its print clearly visible. A second person's phone shows the video anchored over that print. The fabric outside the phone view remains unchanged. This is an illustrative representation, not real filmed product proof.

Show two or three separate moments featuring different chosen videos on the same T-shirt: for example, an original animation, a personal hobby clip, and a shared memory. Use an obvious edit between moments, not continuous rotation that suggests the content changes automatically. The point is different sides of one person, not a wardrobe of different prints.

This is an asset brief, not a produced asset or approved marker concept. Use authorised footage and a validated garment/marker asset when available. If a review mockup uses a placeholder or composite, label it clearly; it cannot establish real tracking performance.

## Division of work

| Element | Job |
|---|---|
| Headline | Establish the relationship between a fixed print and changing content |
| Supporting line | Connect that product behavior to the many sides of a person |
| Body | Make the video concrete, suggest reasons to choose one, and explain where playback is seen |
| Media | Show a desirable T-shirt, phone-only playback, and different choices over time |
| Actions | Let a ready visitor buy or a curious visitor learn the actual steps |

The copy does not rely on video playback to explain the phone view. Use a clear poster image/reduced-motion alternative; write final alt text against the actual asset, not this imagined scene. Check fit in the existing desktop and mobile hero before approval; do not shrink type or add a section to force it in.

## Review and publication checks

- Identity is plural without implying an avatar or requiring the video to feature the wearer.
- Love, creativity, and memory are examples, not demographic assumptions.
- “Through their phone's camera” prevents the embedded-screen interpretation.
- The hero does not claim pointing alone starts playback. The existing how-it-works section must cover QR → link → camera/permission → print → video.
- “Change it whenever you choose” describes the intended owner action, subject to normal service availability and product limits; it is not offline, automatic, or indefinite service. Validate the owner flow before publication.
- “No app needed” means no separate InRL app download, not universal device support or no camera permission.
- Buy now must point to a real purchasable launch offer; See how it works must use the existing section/destination. Availability and destinations are not verified in this document.
- Do not insert future Fandom/Partner offers, capture features, unverified upload support, or final marker artwork.

## How it works

### 01 — Buy your T-shirt

Choose your style, colour, and size.

### 02 — Add your video

Create your account at checkout. Your T-shirt will be waiting there—add the video you want to share and change it whenever you choose.

### 03 — Let others discover your video

People scan the QR code on your T-shirt with their phone, open the experience, and watch your video come to life over the print through their camera.

**No app needed.**

**CTA:** Buy now
